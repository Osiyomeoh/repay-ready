Why check first

A crash doesn't wait for you to wake up

  1. Evening

    You arm a trigger and log off

    Kite's auto-repay fires once, when your health factor falls below the trigger you set. Its repay has to land before the health factor falls below 1.00, when the loan can be liquidated.

  2. Night

    The market falls while you sleep

    On Oct 10, 2025, ETH hit its low at 21:20:25 UTC, 19.12% under noon. That was 10:20 pm in London and Lagos and 5:20 am in Singapore. 304 of the night's 351 USDC liquidations on Aave v3 Base fell between 20:50 and 21:40 UTC.

  3. Before you arm it

    So check it first

    Repay Ready replays your loan and trigger through every price Aave used that night, then answers ARM, ADJUST or REFUSE. When it can't be sure, the answer is REFUSE.
