A first run the same afternoon found a bug in the live check, now fixed:
- src/aave.ts rebuilt the health factor with Aave's pre-v3.5 formula (a rounded-down average liquidation threshold, debt rounded down).
- Aave v3.5.0 and later compute wadDiv(sum of collateral_i x LT_i, totalDebt) / 10000, with debt rounded up (mulDivCeil):
  https://github.com/aave-dao/aave-v3-origin/blob/v3.5.0/src/contracts/protocol/libraries/logic/GenericLogic.sol
- On loans whose collateral legs have different liquidation thresholds the old rebuild missed Aave's HF by more than the 1e-6 tolerance
  (3.5e-5, 6.0e-5 and 8.9e-5 on three loans probed at 12:00 UTC), so the check refused them as "cannot reproduce".
  In that first run, 123 of the 278 USDC-only loans failed the match at 12:00 UTC, all with two or more collateral legs. Example: 0x1c4E6eb654C8d7ddFB241831cB173b113E817a8C (WETH + cbBTC) at block 36,654,126,
  Aave HF 1.0260549926172287; the old rebuild gave 1.0259934476806738 (gap 6.0e-5); the fixed rebuild gives 1.0260549926172287 exactly (test/aave.test.ts).
- The run below is after the fix: 0 USDC-only loans fail the match.
